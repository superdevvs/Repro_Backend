## Demonstration checklist

1. **Authorization:** delegated user, company, scope limits, offline grant, RS512 client key, refresh rotation and revocation. Confirm exact sandbox endpoint and how application installation is approved.
2. **Ordinary flow:** which operation associates company/customer/unit/Job/Place with the correct live Zillow listing? A new Job alone is insufficient. Show recipient and order effects before delivery.
3. **Showcase flow:** locate and fulfill the existing request without creating a duplicate destination. Distinguish API-covered steps from those needing the browser bridge.
4. **Delivery scope:** can the provider bind a send to selected immutable asset IDs or isolate editing? If not, agree on named pilot destinations and no-external-editing windows. Explicitly acknowledge the residual race.
5. **Interrupted response:** accept a write, drop the response, recover its exact ID, and prove that another delivery/email did not occur. Repeat for ingest, attachment and delivery.
6. **Media benefits:** verify ingestion, listing association and product eligibility separately by format/provider. Identify interactive floor-plan/tour requirements and who supplies staging room classification.
7. **Correction/removal:** show cancellation during ingestion, staged cleanup, correction after delivery and retraction after publication; explain retained originals and support response times.
8. **Notifications/commercial:** observe actual agent/coordinator emails, portal/account needs, updates/resends, invoicing/order effects, billing units, rights/withdrawal, beta changes and approved claims.
