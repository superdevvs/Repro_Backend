# Disabled two-adult Lifestyle recipe

This isolated candidate has a closed internal registration but is disabled by default.
It is not deployed, enabled or validated for real-scene production quality.
The public preset remains **Lifestyle**. Its first internal recipe supports exactly
two fictional standing adults conversing on one visibly level source floor region.
It does not claim crowds, seated people, events, arbitrary props or cross-view consistency.

The implemented bounded chain is source-owned framing from the original panorama and six
directions, free extraction, actual-cut and original-parent-context readiness,
one source-only image, actual-person placement/anatomy/optics assessment, one
original-floor SAM request, one candidate-people SAM request, people-specific
support, native composition and independent whole-sphere quality evaluation.
Each paid child uses the persisted workflow-step UUID and existing ambiguous
submission holds. No manual mask, box, pose or successful analysis assertion is accepted.

The first route holds on existing people, seated poses, props, uncertain body
scale/anatomy, missing or hidden shoes, person-person or foreground overlap,
relevant glass/mirror dependencies, insufficient original context, directional
cast shadows or uncertain support. Existing furniture may remain elsewhere; it
must not interact with or occlude the new group. The whole group must retain a
10% cut margin. A generated candidate's actual placement is checked independently
of the proposed source pose.

The support implementation reuses deterministic contour, projected-gravity
and native scalar-shadow primitives. It does not reuse furniture extremum/leg
heuristics or encode people as sofa/chair/table. Both shoes per person require
explicit image-evidenced sole segments, then bounded mask/source-floor checks.
Small original-background holes and visible hand/hair boundaries are retained;
ambiguous topology holds. Only tiny local contact attenuation is permitted.

Source framing and readiness are model visual evidence, not measured depth or
camera calibration. Native output canvas size is not generated detail resolution.
Assisted historical people masks are calibration/negative evidence only.

runtime manifests absent until exact-tree fake-provider and resource proofs pass.
